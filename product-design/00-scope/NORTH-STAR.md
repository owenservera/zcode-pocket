# Product North Star — 2026-10-12

## Vision (USER-CONFIRMED)

Personal Android-first interface to an existing, functioning local organization of ZCode bots over Tailscale. Speaking with the Chief of Staff, Oracle or another bot should feel inviting, natural, lively and simple — not like operating a sterile corporate dashboard. The organization may be very large, but everyday use should remain effortless.

The full long-term vision includes receiving content from anywhere on the phone, acting as an idea partner, preserving context locally and enabling cross-device work. These are **not the current implementation brief**.

## Phase now: BOTS ONLY

Design the mobile *interaction surface* for people talking to bots:
- Individual bot conversations, voice/text entry and attachments.
- Recognizable bot identity and restrained visual personality.
- Bot discovery, contact switching, group and multi-bot conversations.
- Clear observed activity/handoffs/approvals; optional drill-down into collaboration.
- Navigation and menu affordances validated against user-provided Grok Bot mobile reference.
- Experiments comparing minimal messenger, characterful world and hybrid interfaces.

### Non-goals (USER-CONFIRMED)

Do not design or modify local bot provisioning, Oracle, ZCode, machine tooling, agents, models, orchestration, backend protocols, permission enforcement, server data storage or networking. Those systems are separately owned and assumed working.

Saved Views are **parked**. Don't implement them in the bot-only phase.

### Interaction rule

Simple surface first. Progressive disclosure: (1) talk to a bot; (2) see who's helping; (3) examine true observable activity in an optional detail/Backstage surface. 'Backstage' is a working label, not an approved screen.
