# Bot Interaction — UX Working Proposal

## What the owner wants (USER-CONFIRMED)

A lively, engaging and immediately comprehensible way to converse with an entire large organization of existing bots. Most interactions should be as easy as talking to one's Chief of Staff or a specific specialist. Deep bot-to-bot work should remain inspectable without cluttering everyday conversations. Oracle already has privileged control on the local machine; this document does not design Oracle or the back end.

## Concept: familiar faces, organization beneath

Start from a friendly conversation screen rather than a professional analytics console. Recognizable bot avatars, warm but restrained visual identities, readable messages and great mobile input experience. A bot may have a distinctive character without turning the app into a game or pretending to have feelings.

Design priorities:
1. Immediate access to a key bot/conversation.
2. Text and voice entry with low-friction photo, file and image attachment affordances.
3. Distinct personal chat vs multi-bot room, with unmistakable attribution.
4. Contextual activity: compact meaningful progress, collaborators, blocks, permissions and results.
5. Large bot directory/search without flooding the opening UI.
6. Familiar navigation, keyboard-safe fixed composer and one-handed ergonomics.
7. Character motion connected to real observed status; reduced-motion option.

## Progressive disclosure (PROPOSED)

- **Talk**: simple conversation; small bot identity and current status.
- **Who's helping**: tap a specific handoff/activity to see participants and meaningful progress.
- **Backstage**: optional chronological details and artifacts based only on actual observable event records.

## UI surfaces to prototype (not final architecture)

- Inbox/conversation picker.
- One-to-one conversation, including new and streaming message cases.
- Compact bot identity/profile sheet.
- Group conversation and member detail.
- Inline collaboration summary, expandable status and optional detailed activity.
- User-visible permission/approval affordance (render only existing permission semantics).

## Visual directions — OPEN

A. Modern living messenger (subtle, effortless).
B. Character-led environment (strongly expressive).
C. Hybrid: minimalist conversation with rich optional personality/depth.

Hybrid was an assistant suggestion, NOT selected by the owner.

## Things not to do

No new bot creation, tools installation, agent memory design, server redesign, autosync engine, model wiring, authority control, or fake bot event displays. Do not treat screenshots as verified services.
